## 6. Residual risks

- Font substitution: a commercial original font replaced by an open face may never give exact line counts, and a one-line difference in a content-height section is a `rest` failure; such runs end `STUCK` with gate 1 `FAIL`, honestly reported.
- Live-site dependence: the swap probe, target validation and gate 2 need live reads of the original; if it blocks after recon they are `NOT_RUN`, and gate 15 then fails because no entry can move to `content`. A HAR replay would avoid this but would download the original's fonts, which rule 4 forbids.
- The gate 6 CSS-run heuristic (5 identical consecutive declarations) can flag common boilerplate and is evaded by reordering; it is a backstop, not proof.
- The name check skips sentence-initial words, so an invented name at the start of a drafted sentence can pass; the client's approval of every drafted slot is the remaining guard.
- The dHash "inconclusive" class relies on the manifest's provenance being honest.
- Workload: 14.4k words, four viewports plus 320 px, two builds, probe, motion gate on both, production loop, polish loop, three engines and 196 rows is very large for one unattended session.
- The CCPA display duty rests on a secondary source; Firefox and WebKit claims were not run here.
