# Standards and policy review, 2026-10-05

This review checks the relevance, priority and internal consistency of every catalogue row. It does not reperform 275 website tests, certify a client, establish every jurisdiction's law, or freshly validate every historical numeric citation. Existing source notes remain provenance; dated statistics are benchmarks, not universal requirements. Tier assignment is engineering judgment. The following primary sources were checked for the foundational distinctions.

| Primary source | What it supports | Checklist use |
|---|---|---|
| [W3C WCAG 2.2](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/) | Testable, technology-independent accessibility criteria; complete processes and noninterference matter. | A/AA requirements and essential access stay Diamond. Automated scores alone cannot demonstrate conformance. Stricter decorative or AAA preferences must not be confused with AA requirements. |
| [OWASP ASVS](https://owasp.org/projects/asvs) | Technical security verification, including injection defenses; version-specific references recommended. | SEC and applicable BACK controls use actual attack surface. No claim of ASVS certification from this catalogue. |
| [Google Web Vitals](https://web.dev/articles/vitals) | Good field thresholds: p75 LCP 2.5 s, INP 200 ms, CLS 0.1, segmented by mobile/desktop. | SPD-21 remains field evidence, distinct from lab runs. Lighthouse 98 and subsecond desktop targets are house budgets, not universal Diamond safety criteria. |
| [California DOJ privacy guidance](https://oag.ca.gov/sites/all/files/agweb/pdfs/cybersecurity/making_your_privacy_practices_public.pdf) | CalOPPA privacy disclosures depend on collection and covered users. | Actual data flows and jurisdiction determine duties; a cookieless frontend does not prove no personal data processing. |
| [ICO storage/access guidance](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/direct-marketing-and-privacy-and-electronic-communications/guidance-on-the-use-of-storage-and-access-technologies/) | Updated April 2026 guidance includes defined exceptions and requirements beyond a simple essential/nonessential label. | LEG-09 requires a current, jurisdiction-specific consent/exception decision. UK exceptions are not automatically EU or US rules. |
| [FTC advertising and marketing](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/advertising-marketing) | Claims require substantiation; endorsements and reviews must be truthful. | Truthful identity, portfolio claims, reviews and pricing remain Diamond. |

The four tier names are an internal operating system. Neither these sources nor a passing validator awards a Diamond, Gold, Silver or Bronze certificate. Legal obligations are mandatory where applicable; legal uncertainty is unresolved evidence, not permission. Professional legal advice is needed when the question requires it, especially disputed rights, regulated activity or unclear applicability. Requiring a paid lawyer for every unambiguous basic website is an agency policy choice, not a universal statute.
