# Tier policy review, 2026-10-05

The former G/C/R scheme combined applicability, importance and evidence timing. Strict aesthetic and laboratory targets could block launch for the same reason as an exposed secret, while future measurements were mixed with prelaunch obligations. This change separates those concerns without removing evidence history or stable row IDs.

- Every original ID is retained and individually classified with a rationale and timing in `checklist/tiers.json`. The canonical checklist carries a fingerprinted Tier column; `TIERS.md` is the shorter operating view.
- Diamond covers the essential user journey, security, privacy, applicable law, accessibility, truthful content, ownership and safe release. Applicable failures cannot be waived. Gold adds experience and delivery quality. Silver and Bronze retain enhancements and specialist work. Contracted scope still matters at every tier.
- Launch and Diamond phases require Diamond; Gold requires Diamond plus Gold. Silver adds Silver and Bronze adds the remaining catalogue. These names identify priority scopes, not descending safety ratings or external certifications.
- Missing evidence stays unresolved. Conditional N/A needs an observed predicate. Hashes, artifact identity, timestamps, coverage, named human observations and follow-up remain checked at all tiers. Future observations cannot be manufactured before they happen.
- Historical four-column snapshots retain historical semantics. Mixed snapshots fail closed. A new checklist version is not a retroactive pass for an old site record.

|---|---|

## Verification scope

The [source review](SOURCES.md) checks the foundational standards and identifies house policies. The [row review](ROW_REVIEW.md) records mixed requirements and retained overlap. The [independent review](INDEPENDENT_REVIEW.md) challenges the executable changes. Repository structural and mutation checks validate the kit; they do not certify a deployed website. Historical source statistics and all jurisdiction-specific legal claims have not been freshly re-researched individually.
